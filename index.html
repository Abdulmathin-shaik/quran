Tajweed Color Code Rules

Madd Lazim (Compulsory 6 Vowels)
Prolong for 6 vowel counts (harakat). Occurs when a madd letter is followed by a permanent sukun or shaddah.
Madd Wajib (Obligatory 4-5 Vowels)
Prolong for 4 or 5 vowel counts. Occurs when a madd letter is followed by a hamzah.
Madd Ja'iz / Arid Li-s-Sukun (2, 4 or 6 Vowels)
Permissible prolongation for 2, 4, or 6 counts before stopping on a temporary sukun.
Madd Normal / Asli (2 Vowels)
Natural 2 vowel counts prolongation for Alif, Waw, or Ya without hamzah or sukun following.
Ghunnah, Ikhfa & Idgham with Ghunnah
Nasalization sound held for 2 vowel counts on Noon/Meem with shaddah or Ikhfa concealment.
Qalqalah (Echo / Rebound)
Bouncing or echoing reverberation on letters of Qutb Jad (ق ط ب ج د) when bearing sukun or stopping.
Hamzatul Wasl & Silent Letters
Letters written in script but unpronounced during continuous recitation (e.g. connecting Wasl, solar Lam).
